Time pressure tends to shape a psychedelic experience, and people often notice it
more in hindsight than in advance. It is worth looking at what is driving
the clock before anything begins.

You may want to consider:

- Do you have the day, and ideally the day after, free?
- Is anyone waiting on you, or expecting you somewhere?
- What is the plan if you want more time than you scheduled?
- Where can you rest afterward without needing to perform or travel?

Less rushed does not mean an easy time is guaranteed. It means fewer outside
pressures pulling on you while you are less able to manage them.
