Some experiences leave a loose thread — a feeling, an image, or a question
that has not settled.

There is no deadline to resolve it, and no rule that it must mean something.
Sitting with something unresolved is not the same as something being wrong.

That said: if an unsettled feeling has been going on for more than a few
weeks, or if it is getting in the way of daily life — sleep, work,
relationships — that is worth talking through with a qualified professional.
Not because something is wrong with you, but because you deserve actual
support, and that is what it is for.

If you want to make notes for yourself, you can. You can also skip it.
