If someone is preparing for an experience and you may be the one who
stays, a few plain things are worth settling in the days before — for
them, and for you.

You may want to consider: what has been agreed about what you are and are
not present for; who else could be called if you need a break; and what
your own limits are. Supporting someone does not require you to share the
experience, or to have a strong feeling about it.

Your limits are part of the plan, not a failure in it. Settled ahead of
time, nobody has to negotiate them mid-event.
