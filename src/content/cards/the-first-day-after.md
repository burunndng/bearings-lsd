The first day after doesn't need to be productive, and it doesn't need to
contain any conclusions. It's fine for it to just be a day of rest.

Simple, practical things that tend to help:

- Water and food that doesn't need much from you.
- Sleep, or at least lying down, even if sleep doesn't come easily right
  away.
- Low light and low noise, if either feels like too much.
- Being around someone if that helps, or having space if that helps more.

If something feels off in a way that isn't settling — physically or
emotionally — [Support & safety](/safety) is always reachable, today or any
day.

There's no schedule to keep and no lesson due. Rest is a complete use of
the day on its own.
