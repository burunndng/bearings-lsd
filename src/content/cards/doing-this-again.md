There's nothing wrong with wanting to do this again. Curiosity, connection,
wonder, or just enjoying something — none of that needs a second
justification.

One thing worth being honest with yourself about: is the reason you're
reaching for it sooner than you'd planned, or is it specifically to avoid
something — a feeling, a conversation, a problem that hasn't gone away?
That's not a diagnosis, and it doesn't mean anything is wrong with you. It's
just a distinction worth noticing. The two reasons can point in
different directions, and it can be worth knowing which one is in the
driver's seat before anything else happens.

If avoidance is part of what's going on, [talking with a qualified
clinician](/safety) is a reasonable next step — not because you've done
something wrong, but because that's exactly what real support is for.
