I had a good time. That is enough.

Not every experience needs to become a lesson, a breakthrough, or a turning
point. You are allowed to have simply been there, enjoyed it, and to leave it
at that.

If something surfaces later that you want to look at, it will still be there.
And if nothing does, that is not a missed opportunity. A good time is a
complete thing on its own.
