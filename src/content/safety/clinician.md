Some situations are worth discussing with a qualified clinician first. This is
not a complete medical screening, and this app cannot assess you. Anyone with
a complex medical or psychiatric history should speak with a professional.

It is worth a conversation if you have, or a close family member has:

- A personal or family history of psychosis, mania, or bipolar disorder.
- A heart condition, or take medication that affects the heart.
- Taken lithium or an MAOI — some combinations carry serious risk. A case
  series has linked classic psychedelics taken alongside lithium specifically
  (not lamotrigine) with seizures.
- Been changing psychiatric medication, especially antidepressants.
- Any medical condition you are unsure about, or you are pregnant.

If your usual doctor is not the right person to ask, harm-reduction services
and psychedelic-informed clinician directories can help you find someone who
is. See the resources below.
