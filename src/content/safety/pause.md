There is no cost to pausing, and no deadline. If any of this fits your
situation, waiting or reconsidering is a reasonable choice — not a failure.

You may want to pause if:

- You feel pressured by someone else to go ahead.
- You are in an unfamiliar or unsafe place, or with people you do not trust.
- You are in acute distress right now, or have been having a very hard time.
- You are unsure about a medication, a medical condition, or mixing substances.
- Nobody knows where you are, or when to expect to hear from you.
- You do not have a free day after this, to rest without needing to perform.
- What you have has not been tested, and you do not know what is actually in it.
- Something simply feels off and you cannot say why.

Pausing keeps every option open. Going ahead closes some of them.
