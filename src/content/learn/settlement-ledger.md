Some insights arrive vivid and certain. Whether they still feel that way in
an ordinary week is a different question. A settlement ledger is a simple
way to watch that — not to score progress, but to let time do part of the
work that reflection alone cannot.

Nothing here is required. You can use this for one insight or none, on paper
or in your [notes](/notes).

## The dated log — three columns

Pick one insight or image to follow — one row is enough. Revisit it on three
occasions you choose: for example, the next day, a week later, and a month
later.

| When | Felt true at the time | Still hums now | What ordinary life says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day +1 | What felt most true right after — in its own words? | How does it feel to recall it now — vivid, quiet, distant, charged? | What does an ordinary Tuesday suggest — sleep, work, the people around you? |
| Week +1 | — | — | — |
| Month +1 | — | — | — |

Some people find the third column the most useful. If an insight felt
expansive in the moment but ordinary life keeps saying "not yet" or
"not this way," that disagreement is worth noticing. If all three columns
point the same way over weeks, that is also worth noticing. Both are
complete outcomes — an insight that fades is not a failure.

You may want to consider writing only what happened and how it felt, and
leaving what it means alone for now. Meaning tends to be more useful when it
arrives on its own.

## The weekly check — two questions

Once a week, if you want, ask two plain questions:

**1. Is daily life basically holding?**

- Sleep, eating, showing up where you need to show up
- Caring for yourself or for someone who depends on you
- Whether using alcohol or other things to manage how you feel is creeping up

If any of these have been off for more than a couple of weeks, that is not a
sign that you failed to integrate — it is a sign that the experience has not
finished settling, and that professionals can genuinely help with that.

**2. Is this insight making more room, or needing more specialness?**

A rough way to notice: insights that are settling tend to make ordinary life
feel a little more workable — a bit more choice, a bit more patience with one
actual person, a bit more tolerance for not knowing. Insights that are mostly
being held tend to need repetition, special language, or admiration to stay
alive, and ordinary life feels more contracted by comparison. Neither is a
verdict; both are information you can return to.

You may want to consider noting the answers as a single line per week, not a
score. There is no total to compute and no threshold to pass. Some peaks
remain peaks — vivid waypoints that do not need to become traits.

## How this fits with the other lenses

The [four lenses](/learn/four-lenses-snapshot) page captures an experience at
a single moment. This ledger watches that same material over time. You can
use either, both, or neither. If the ledger starts to feel like a task with a
deadline, you may want to consider setting it aside — waiting keeps every
option open.
