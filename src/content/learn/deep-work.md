Most of this shelf is evidence-first: what is known, how well it is known,
and what it cannot promise. The three pieces gathered here sit one step
further out. They borrow from traditions that do not agree with each other —
analytic psychology, integral theory, transpersonal psychology, Hermetic
symbolism — and they are offered as ways of looking, not as claims about
what is real.

They are for the smaller case: an experience that keeps asking for
attention after the first weeks, and you want somewhere more structured to
put that attention. Nothing here is a next step, and skipping all of it is
a complete answer.

- [Four lenses for looking back](/learn/four-lenses-snapshot) — image,
  shape, state, symbol, and one plain question for an ordinary week.
- [A doorway that keeps returning](/card/doorway) — capture a single
  returning image without deciding what it means.
- [The settlement ledger](/learn/settlement-ledger) — watch an insight land
  over time: what felt true, what still hums, what ordinary life says.

If any of this starts to feel like pressure — a duty to get something from
the experience — that is the sign to put it down. The experience is not
owed a project.
