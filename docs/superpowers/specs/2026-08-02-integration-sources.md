## Papers

1. **Bathje, G. J., Majeski, E., & Kudowor, M. (2022).** Psychedelic
   integration: An analysis of the concept and its practice. *Frontiers in
   Psychology, 13*, 824077. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.824077
   — The definitional anchor: revisiting, making sense of, working through,
   incorporating. Catalogues practices; cautions against impulsive changes
   and premature evangelizing.

2. **Gorman, I., Nielson, E. M., Molinar, A., Cassidy, K., & Sabbagh, J.
   (2021).** Psychedelic Harm Reduction and Integration: A transtheoretical
   model for clinical practice. *Frontiers in Psychology, 12*, 645246.
   https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.645246

3. **Evans, J., Robinson, O. C., Argyri, E. K., et al. (2023).** Extended
   difficulties following the use of psychedelic drugs: A mixed methods
   study. *PLOS ONE, 18*(10), e0293349.
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0293349 — N=608; ~30% of affected
   users >1 year, ~15% >3 years. Basis for the "when it's not settling" card.

4. **Robinson, O. C., et al. (2024).** Coming back together: a qualitative
   survey study of coping and support strategies used by people to cope with
   extended difficulties after the use of psychedelic drugs. *Frontiers in
   Psychology, 15*, 1369715. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1369715
   — Percentages (self-report, N=608): peer/community support 34%;
   meditation **and prayer** 27%; reading 14%; exercise 12%; breathing
   strategies 11%; journaling 11%; embodied contemplative practices
   (yoga/tai chi) 10%; time in nature 10%. Do NOT conflate breathing and
   yoga into one number.

5. **Gandy, S., Forstmann, M., Carhart-Harris, R. L., Timmermann, C.,
   Luke, D., & Watts, R. (2020).** The potential synergistic effects between
   psychedelic administration and nature contact for the improvement of
   mental health. *Health Psychology Open, 7*(2), 2055102920978123.
   https://doi.org/10.1177/2055102920978123

6. **Watts, R., & Luoma, J. B. (2020).** The use of the psychological
   flexibility model to support psychedelic assisted therapy (ACE model).
   *Journal of Contextual Behavioral Science, 15*, 92–102.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcbs.2019.12.004

## Resources

7. **Fireside Project** — https://firesideproject.org — free, confidential
   US peer support line, call/text 623-473-7433 (62-FIRESIDE), 7 days/week.
   Outcome data (Pleet et al., 2023, *Psychedelic Medicine*): 65.9% of
   callers de-escalated from psychological distress; 29.3% said harm likely
   averted; 12.5% might have called 911; 10.8% might have gone to the ER.
   ⚠️ Exact daily hours not confirmable from their JS-rendered site
   (third-party listings say 11am–11pm PT) — verify before publishing hours.
   ⚠️ Do not use older 82–88% pilot figures; superseded.

8. **Psychedelic.Support** — https://psychedelic.support — therapist/provider
   directory (psychedelic.support/network), free to browse. ⚠️ No standalone
   integration-circle directory confirmed on the site; circles are largely
   listed via community aggregators (e.g. Global Psychedelic Society,
   Fireside's resource database). Do not claim a circles directory here.

9. **MAPS Integration Station** — https://maps.org/integration-station/ —
   free integration education, the MAPS Integration Workbook, and the Bathje
   et al. Synthesized Model.

10. **Zendo Project** — https://zendoproject.org — psychedelic harm-reduction
    education, Zendo SIT (Sitting and Integration Training), event peer
    support.
